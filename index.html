Rules and Terms of Agreement

Read these guidelines before starting your evaluation.

Evaluation guidelines

01
Honesty and fairness

Rate actual contributions, not friendship, popularity, assumptions, or personal disagreements.

02
Focus on observable actions

Consider completed tasks, responsibility, cooperation, participation, and communication during the project.

03
No retaliation or score manipulation

Do not intentionally give low ratings to punish a classmate or coordinate ratings with others.

04
Give constructive feedback

Comments must be respectful, relevant, and based on observed behavior. Personal attacks are not acceptable.

05
Understand the grading policy

Peer evaluation may inform a deduction of up to 2 points from the original 20-point activity score, subject to teacher review.

06
Respect privacy

Do not share screenshots or evaluations to embarrass classmates. Complete evaluations independently.

⚑ Important grading reminder

Peer ratings are supporting evidence, not an automatic grade. The teacher reviews low ratings and supporting evidence before approving any deduction.

✓ Academic integrity
