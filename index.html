STEP 1 · AGREEMENT

Before You Begin

Keep these three reminders in mind when evaluating your teammates.

01
Be fair and honest.

Rate actual contributions, not friendships or personal disagreements.

02
Focus on contributions.

Consider completed tasks, responsibility, cooperation, and participation.

03
Evaluate independently.

Do not coordinate ratings or use feedback to retaliate against classmates.

ⓘ Important reminder

Peer ratings support your teacher’s review. They do not automatically determine a classmate’s grade. Any deduction requires teacher review and supporting evidence.

Student guide

Help & Guide

Follow these steps to submit a fair, complete peer evaluation.

1

How to complete an evaluation

Your answers are saved only after you review and submit.

  1. Read the agreement. Review the reminders and check the box to confirm that you will evaluate fairly and independently.
  2. Enter your information. Confirm your Zobel account email, name, campus, and section. Choose the classmate you are evaluating.
  3. Rate the five criteria. Select one rating for each criterion based on what you observed during the group work.
  4. Write useful feedback. Describe one specific strength and one respectful area for improvement.
  5. Review and submit. Check the classmate, ratings, and comments, then confirm submission. You may submit up to two evaluations, one per classmate.
2

Rating scale

Use the same scale for each of the five criteria.

RatingLevelHow to interpret it
4ExcellentConsistently shows the contribution described by the criterion.
3ProficientUsually shows the contribution described by the criterion.
2DevelopingShows some contribution, but it is inconsistent or needs reminders.
1BeginningShows limited contribution to the criterion.

Criteria: Task Contribution; Responsibility; Cooperation; Participation and Initiative; Communication and Accountability.

3

How the evaluation percentage is calculated

There are five ratings, each worth 1 to 4 points. The highest possible total is 20.

Formula

Total of the five ratings ÷ 20 × 100 = evaluation percentage

Example: Ratings of 4, 3, 3, 4, and 2 add up to 16.

16 ÷ 20 × 100 = 80%

4

How a suggested score adjustment works

This is a teacher review guide. Peer ratings do not automatically change a classmate’s grade.

Average peer ratingSuggested adjustment on a 20-point activityNext step
90–100%0 pointsNo deduction suggested
80–89.9%0.5 pointTeacher reviews evidence
70–79.9%1 pointTeacher reviews evidence
60–69.9%1.5 pointsTeacher reviews evidence
Below 60%No automatic deductionInvestigate the evidence first

Example: If the original score is 18/20 and the peer average is 80%, the suggested adjustment is 0.5 point: 18 − 0.5 = 17.5/20.

For assignments with a different total, the suggested adjustment scales in proportion to the assignment total. A teacher must review the evidence and approve any official change.
